Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you visit Roofing Plainfield (roofingplainfield.com) or call the telephone number displayed on this website.

Roofing Plainfield is an independently operated informational and lead-generation website. It helps homeowners seek connections with independent roofing service providers; it does not itself perform roofing work.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the website and telephone service, information that may be collected includes:

  • Information you choose to provide when requesting assistance or discussing a roofing project.
  • Your telephone number, caller ID information, and basic call details, such as the time or duration of an inquiry, where available.
  • Details you share during a telephone inquiry, such as your general location, service needs, and project preferences.
  • Basic technical information associated with visits to the website, such as IP address, browser type, and requested pages, if collected by the website's hosting infrastructure.

The current homepage does not contain a contact form or built-in analytics or advertising scripts. Other information collection may depend on the website host or telephone service used.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, connect callers with relevant independent service providers, facilitate requested service discussions or consultations, assess and improve website performance and inquiry quality, help prevent misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Lead Generation and Third-Party Service Providers

This website operates for lead-generation purposes. If you call the number shown on the website, your inquiry may be routed or referred to an independent third-party roofing business or service provider that may be able to assist you.

The website operator may receive compensation for qualifying calls or referrals. The providers are independent businesses responsible for their own services, pricing, licensing, insurance, and privacy practices. Calling the website does not, by itself, mean that you consent to unrelated marketing calls or messages.

5. Call Tracking and Recording

Telephone inquiries may be routed or tracked through third-party telecommunications or call-handling services to connect callers and account for referrals. Call details, such as caller ID, timing, duration, and routing information, may be processed through those services.

Whether calls are recorded has not been confirmed. If call recording is enabled, callers should receive any notice and opportunity to consent required by applicable law. This policy does not represent that all calls are recorded.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

The supplied homepage does not include built-in cookie-setting analytics, advertising pixels, or other tracking scripts. The hosting provider may nevertheless process standard technical information needed to serve the website. If cookies or analytics tools are added later, this policy should be updated to explain their use.

7. Information Sharing

Information related to your inquiry may be shared with a relevant independent service provider so that the provider can respond to or discuss your request. Information may also be processed by technical service providers supporting website hosting, telephone routing, call handling, or related operations, as applicable.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to address fraud, security concerns, or misuse. No additional sharing arrangements are asserted here.

8. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect information from unauthorized access or misuse, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Information should be retained only as reasonably necessary for its purpose and applicable obligations. Specific retention periods and safeguards depend on the website operator and its service providers and have not been confirmed.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information. How a request can be fulfilled may depend on the information held by the website operator and by independent providers.

A dedicated privacy contact method has not yet been supplied. The website operator should publish an appropriate contact method for privacy inquiries before launch. For information held by an independent service provider, you may also need to contact that provider directly.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

Independent service providers, telephone service operators, and third-party websites may collect or process information under their own privacy policies. Roofing Plainfield does not control the privacy practices of those independent parties.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised if the website, its telephone referral arrangements, or information-handling practices change. The updated version will appear on this page with a revised date.

This website is independent of the former Allender & Sons business and is not affiliated with that former company.
